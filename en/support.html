FAQ

How do I get Trackmade Premium?

In the app under Appearance → Trackmade Premium. Try it free for 7 days, then buy Premium once – no subscription.

I bought Premium but it isn’t showing.

Open the purchase screen and tap “Restore Purchases”. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

How do I back up my data?

Turn on iCloud backup in the app (menu “…” → iCloud Backup). Trackmade then backs up to your iCloud once a day. On a new iPhone, Trackmade offers to load the backup on first launch.

How do I add a widget to my Home Screen?

Touch and hold your Home Screen → “+” → Trackmade → choose a size. Then touch and hold the widget → “Edit Widget” → choose a module.

How do I change the language?

Trackmade follows your iPhone’s language. To change it for Trackmade only, go to Settings → Apps → Trackmade → Language.

What are shortcuts?

Shortcuts do things automatically, following the pattern “When … then …”. For example: when you log a run, the day is checked off in your habit module – or Trackmade reminds you at 8 pm if you haven’t logged anything today. You find them in a module under “…” → Shortcuts.

How do I rearrange tiles or blocks?

Home screen: menu “…” → Edit Home Screen, then press and hold a tile briefly and drag it to its place. In the module editor it works the same: hold a block briefly, then drag. Swiping without holding scrolls as usual.

How do I change the size?

In edit mode, every tile and block has a rounded corner at the bottom right. Drag it – the size snaps to the grid. For blocks you can also tap the block → Size.

I removed a block by accident.

Tap “Undo” at the bottom right away. You can still get it back later: add the block again – Trackmade then offers to show your earlier entries again. Removing never deletes entries.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your iPhone. To delete iCloud backups, use the Files app under iCloud Drive → Trackmade.
