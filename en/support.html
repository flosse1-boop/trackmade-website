FAQ

How do I get Trackmade Premium?

In the app under Appearance → Trackmade Premium. Try it free for 7 days, then buy Premium once – no subscription.

I bought Premium but it isn’t showing.

Open the purchase screen and tap “Restore Purchases”. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

How do I back up my data?

Turn on iCloud backup in the app (menu “…” → iCloud Backup). Trackmade then backs up to your iCloud once a day. On a new iPhone, Trackmade offers to load the backup on first launch.

How do I add a widget to my Home Screen?

Touch and hold your Home Screen → “+” → Trackmade → choose a size. Then touch and hold the widget → “Edit Widget” → choose a module.

How do I change the language?

Trackmade follows your iPhone’s language. To change it for Trackmade only, go to Settings → Apps → Trackmade → Language.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your iPhone. To delete iCloud backups, use the Files app under iCloud Drive → Trackmade.
