Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

In short: Trackmade has no account, no server of its own, no ads and no tracking. What you enter in the app stays on your iPhone – and, if you turn it on, in your own iCloud. We never see your data.

2. Data in the app

Trackmade stores modules, entries, photos, rules, templates and settings only locally on your device. So that the Home Screen widgets can show the same data, it lives in a storage area shared by the app and its widget on your iPhone. Trackmade does not connect to any server of ours and does not send any content to us or to third parties.

3. iCloud backup (optional)

If you turn on iCloud backup, Trackmade saves a copy of your data to your own iCloud Drive once a day (folder “Trackmade”). It is stored by Apple under your Apple Account; we have no access to it. Apple’s Privacy Policy applies. You can turn the backup off in the app at any time and delete backups in the Files app. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (a feature you requested).

4. Apple Health

With your permission, Trackmade reads the following from Apple Health:

  • for the “Steps & Activity” module: steps, distance, floors, active calories and exercise minutes,
  • for the “Body Weight” module: your body weight, for example from a scale connected to Apple Health. Your weight is also used to estimate walking calories if you don’t have an Apple Watch.

Trackmade never writes to Apple Health. Health data is only displayed on your device; it is not shared or used for advertising. So that Home Screen widgets can show something while your iPhone is locked, Trackmade also keeps the most recently read values in its own storage on your device. This copy is not included in Trackmade’s iCloud backup. Weights you enter yourself in Trackmade are regular entries like in any other module (see section 2). You can revoke access at any time in your iPhone settings under “Health”. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR).

5. Camera and photos

For photo fields, you can take photos or pick them from your library with your permission. Photos are stored only within Trackmade on your device (and in your iCloud backup, if enabled).

6. Notifications

Reminders, for example before subscription charges or for tasks, are scheduled directly on your iPhone. No data is sent to us or to third parties. You can turn notifications off at any time in your iPhone settings.

7. Purchases

You buy Trackmade Premium and the free trial through the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we receive no payment or account details. The app only asks Apple whether a purchase exists. Apple’s Privacy Policy applies.

8. No analytics, no ads

Trackmade contains no ads, no analytics or tracking tools and no third-party software that collects data about you.

9. Crash reports and TestFlight

If you allow sharing analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple provides us with anonymized crash reports. If you test Trackmade via TestFlight, Apple also provides us with your voluntary feedback (including screenshots), crash reports, and the name and email address you were invited with. We use this information only to fix bugs and improve the app. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a bug-free app).

10. This website

trackmade.de is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, date and time, and browser in order to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

11. Contact by email

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to handle your request and delete them once they are no longer needed. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR.

12. Deleting your data

To delete all data in the app, delete Trackmade from your iPhone. To delete iCloud backups, use the Files app under iCloud Drive → Trackmade.

13. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. Since we do not receive your app data, this mainly concerns emails and TestFlight information. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.

14. Changes

If the app or the law changes, we will update this privacy policy. The version published here applies.
